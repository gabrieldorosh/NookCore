# Build a better journey

Ride in the Overworld to enjoy the travel buffs. In normal Survival play, no command or special item is needed.

**Road mounts**
Horses, donkeys, mules, camels, skeleton horses and zombie horses get a 3× movement-speed boost on qualifying surfaces. Every mount gets the same proportional boost: a faster horse stays faster. The boost builds up smoothly and fades after leaving the road, so small gaps are forgiving.

**Your road palette**
- Dirt paths, coarse dirt, podzol, mud and gravel.
- Bricks, cobblestone, mossy cobblestone and stone bricks, including their matching slabs and stairs.
- Cracked stone bricks (full blocks).

Mix these materials freely. The surface matters, not who placed it: matching natural terrain counts too. Ordinary dirt, grass, smooth stone and other unlisted surfaces do not provide the boost. Keep routes wide and clear for your mount, and test slab/stair transitions as you build.

**Railways**
Occupied passenger minecarts can reach 32 blocks per second on suitable powered routes. Give them straight track to accelerate. They slow for upcoming bends, slopes, gaps and unpowered powered rails; 32 is a top speed, not a guaranteed speed everywhere. Farm and cargo carts retain ordinary behaviour.

These travel buffs apply in the Overworld. Creative-mode testing bypasses them. Rocket and elytra behaviour is unchanged in this update.
