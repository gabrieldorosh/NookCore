# On the Right Track
### Niko’s Nook · 30 September 2026

All roads lead somewhere. This week, getting there gets a little quicker.

**TRAVEL**
- Ridden horses, camels, donkeys and mules get a 3× movement-speed boost on supported Overworld road surfaces. Faster mounts keep their advantage, so a good horse is still worth breeding.
- Player-occupied minecarts can reach 32 blocks per second on suitable powered routes, with slowing for bends, slopes and unpowered powered rails.
- Elytra and rocket behaviour stays unchanged for now. We’ll revisit flight when planning the End opening.

**THIS WEEK’S QUESTS**
- Defeat 8 witches.
- Contribute 128 regular rails and 256 oak logs — two separate quests.
- Visit 8 different Overworld biomes.
- Catch or hunt 32 salmon.
- Mine 8 natural diamond ore blocks.
- Weekly challenge: defeat 200 qualifying hostile mobs.

Each regular quest pays 15 Nooks; the challenge pays 30. That’s 120 Nooks for the full set. Check `/quests` for progress and `/quests contribute` for hand-in choices. Donated materials stay with the community.

**A LITTLE HOUSEKEEPING**
- TAB shows Nooks balances, richest first, and server TPS.
- New pastel titles for mr. duck, mrs. bee and mrs. niko.
- Mining, combat and fishing quest progress messages are spaced out; completion still appears immediately.

Weekly quests refresh Wednesdays at 02:00 UK time, alongside the content window.

Build a road. Lay some track. Take the scenic route.
