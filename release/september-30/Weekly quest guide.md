# This week at the Nook

Open `/quests` for your goals and progress. Play in Survival in the Overworld to progress this set. Weekly refresh: Wednesday, 02:00 UK time.

**Six quests · 15 Nooks each**
- Defeat 8 witches.
- Contribute 128 regular rails.
- Contribute 256 oak logs.
- Visit 8 different biomes.
- Catch or hunt 32 salmon.
- Mine 8 natural diamond ore blocks, including deepslate diamond ore. The goal counts blocks, not the number of diamonds Fortune gives you.

**Weekly challenge · 30 Nooks**
Defeat 200 qualifying hostile mobs: zombies, skeletons, spiders, cave spiders, creepers, husks, strays, drowned, witches, phantoms or pillagers. Witch kills count towards both the witch quest and this challenge.

**Handing in materials**
Keep ordinary, unmodified items in your inventory, then use:
- `/quests contribute rail`
- `/quests contribute oak_log`

Or open `/quests contribute` and click your choice. Opening that menu does not take items. You can contribute in smaller batches; each command takes only what is still needed for that quest. Rails and logs have separate progress. These are donations: materials stay with the community, with no refund at the weekly refresh.

The full set pays 120 Nooks. Progress messages for mining, combat and fishing are spaced out; progress still counts between messages and completion is announced immediately.
