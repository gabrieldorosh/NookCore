# Selected quests and effort

Six regular rewards of 15 Nooks, plus a 30-Nook challenge: 120 Nooks total. No paid advancement rewards or prices change.

| Goal | Planning estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 48 natural iron ore | 15–45 minutes | Mining conditions vary; smelting/raw iron cannot substitute. |
| 6 phantoms | Several night cycles | Depends on insomnia, weather, sleeping and local spawning. Not a reliable timed task. |
| 64 carved pumpkins | 5–20 minutes with crops ready | Existing stock can be handed in. Shearing pumpkins supplies the blocks; growing time varies. |
| 8 shulkers | 15–45 minutes after reaching an unlooted End city | City discovery/travel can dominate. Progress is unavailable until the End opens Friday 19:00. |
| 16 end rods | 5–20 minutes with access to an End city/resources | Collect or craft; resource access and already-looted cities vary. Shared community contribution. |
| 16 honey bottles | Several hive harvests | 16 bottles require 16 full-hive collections; multiple hives and existing stock shorten elapsed time. |
| 24 natural diamond ore | 45–120+ minutes | Challenge target, strongly dependent on gear, luck and route. Fortune does not shorten the ore-block count. |

These are rough planning estimates, not measured completion times or promises. Players who join later in the week still have until the following Wednesday 02:00. All progress starts after Niko acceptance; hand-ins can use already-owned materials.
