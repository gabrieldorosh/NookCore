# Selected quests and focused checks

Owner-approved October 7 set. Six regular quests pay 15 Nooks each; challenge pays 30, total 120.

| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Drowned | Defeat 24 drowned |
| Iron | Mine 48 natural iron ore blocks; normal/deepslate both count, Fortune does not multiply progress |
| Pillagers | Defeat 12 pillagers |
| Glowstone | Contribute 32 glowstone BLOCKS, not dust |
| Crops | Harvest 64 fully grown wheat, carrots, potatoes or beetroot blocks |
| Villager trading | Complete 8 transactions with villagers |
| Challenge | Contribute 4 ghast tears |

All tracking remains Survival in the Overworld. Nether materials can be brought back after the Nether opens Friday 9 October; existing owned or traded materials also count. This update does not open the Nether itself. Contributions consume the items and put them into community stock; they are not merely ownership checks. Use /quests contribute glowstone or /quests contribute ghast_tear.

Crops: manually break fully mature plants. One block counts once regardless of drops/Fortune; replanting and growing the same spot again is allowed, including bone meal. Immature plants, water/piston/villager auto-harvesting, pumpkins/melons, cocoa and Nether wart are excluded. Right-click harvesting supplied by another plugin is not supported here. Cancelled/protected breaks must not progress it.

Trading: one paid, completed recipe transaction counts once, regardless of result stack size. Repeating a trade with the same villager is allowed; each completed transaction in a shift-click batch counts. Opening/talking/browsing, cancelled purchases and unavailable offers do not count. Wandering traders and custom shop menus are excluded. This does not require buying from player shops.

## Focused player checks

- Q1: In Survival, break one immature and one mature crop. Only the mature crop adds one. Try another crop species; Fortune must not multiply it. Protected land should reject the break and give no progress.
- Q2: Open a villager, browse an offer, then complete one ordinary trade. Only the completed purchase adds one. Complete a shift-click batch and compare the number of paid transactions with progress; stop at eight. A full inventory/failed purchase must not add phantom progress.
- Q3: Harvest the remaining crops. Rapid harvesting/trading should produce the first progress message and completion, with at most one intermediate notice per ten seconds. Each completed regular goal pays 15 Nooks once. Reconnect/restart and verify progress/reward persist.
- Q4: /quests contribute offers glowstone and ghast_tear separately. Confirm the complete list matches the table; do not sacrifice scarce items just to test before Friday.
- Q5: Existing title/keyword changes: miss bee in yellow; LAG! gets a private tip, rapid repeats do not; normal public chat and Discord relay still work once.

The existing automated protection/cancellation and payout checks pass, but these client checks have not been claimed as completed.

## Future quest direction

Owner wants genuinely different activities, not just changing hand-in materials. Dyeing sheep is a future candidate. Avoid repeating fish/biomes/coal immediately. Generic mob farms reduce hunt difficulty. Do not duplicate entering the Nether/return-to-sender advancement rewards as quests. Mount distance can encourage riding circles and is not a priority. Niko and larger enchantment features are deferred from this small update.
